Living wallpapers for Wayland.

Rinka draws images, videos and converted Wallpaper Engine scenes behind your windows on Niri and Hyprland. Its editor previews them with the same engine, so what you see while choosing is what your monitors show.

Install with Nix Source on GitHub

Rust + wgpu · WebGPU preview · GPL-3.0-or-later · early and moving fast

What it does

One engine for your desktop and your preview.

The renderer is written once in Rust. Natively it draws on Wayland layer-shell surfaces; compiled to WebAssembly it draws the editor's preview through WebGPU.

Identical colors, checked

Preview and wallpaper share one color pipeline, video YUV conversion included. Test frames read back byte-for-byte equal on both paths.

#1e6f73 → rgb(30, 111, 115) on screen

Wallpaper Engine scenes, honestly

Rinka reads workshop packages, converts image layers and translates effect shaders to WGSL. Each import comes with a report of what converted, what is approximated and what is missing.

264 of 277 effect shaders translate

Video that keeps time

FFmpeg decodes, frames are scheduled by their timestamps, variable frame rates play as authored, and hidden outputs stop costing anything.

frames within 1.1 ms of their timestamps

Every monitor, its own wallpaper

Pick displays on a to-scale map of your outputs. Rotated and mixed-resolution setups get correctly sized buffers, and the wallpaper never takes your clicks.

background layer · no input · no focus

The library

Your wallpapers, in one place.

Drop in images and videos, pick solid colors, or import from your Wallpaper Engine workshop. Thumbnails come from the engine itself, so they match what plays.

Install

Try it in one command.

Rinka ships as a Nix flake. It needs a Wayland compositor with wlr-layer-shell (Niri and Hyprland are the tested targets) and a GPU with Vulkan.

Run it

Opens the editor; it starts the wallpaper host on its own.

nix run github:zaroc-dev/rinka

Keep it

With Home Manager, the host starts at login and restores the wallpapers you picked.

# flake.nix
inputs.rinka.url = "github:zaroc-dev/rinka";

# home.nix
imports = [ inputs.rinka.homeManagerModules.default ];
services.rinka.enable = true;

Without Home Manager

Add the package and start the host with your compositor, for example in Niri.

spawn-at-startup "rinka-host" "serve" "--restore"

Status

What works today, and what is next.

Rinka is young. Its milestones come from a written spec, and import priorities come from measuring real workshop scenes rather than guessing.

Works

Colors, images, video

Fit, fill, stretch, center and tile; looping video with pause and seek; per-display assignment.

Works

Scene import with effects

Image layers, transforms and translated effect shaders such as water ripple and shake, with a conversion report.

Next

Particles, solid and composition layers

Particles appear in 28 of 33 measured scenes; solid and composition layers in 13 each.

Later

Text, sound, scripts, Windows

SceneScript, audio response and Windows wallpaper playback are planned as separate steps.
